Marble Maestro — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Marble Maestro is a single-player puzzle game developed by SoundBolt32 (Ayushman Dhar). This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: the game collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Data we collect

None. The game has no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash-reporting SDKs and no in-app purchases.

  • Game progress (stars, unlocked levels, audio volume, mute state) is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. It never leaves the device.
  • No network access is required to play. The Android build may declare the platform-standard INTERNET permission through the Unity engine template, but the game makes no network requests and sends no data anywhere.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, audio, files or any other personal or sensitive data.
  • No data is shared with third parties.

Children's privacy

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13. It contains no ads, no purchases and no social features.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the game removes all stored progress and settings from your device. You can also clear progress at any time in Settings → Reset Progress inside the game.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that change this policy (for example ads), this page will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be noted in the Play Store release notes.
